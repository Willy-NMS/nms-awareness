Security awareness tips

  • Think before you click
  • Spoofing: the name is not the sender
  • New bank details? Call to verify
  • Gift card requests are a scam
  • Never share your code
  • Check the link before you sign in
  • Unexpected attachment? Don't open it
  • Fake "verify" and "fix it" pages
  • Texts and calls can be scams too
  • Something looks wrong? Report it
  • Use strong, unique passwords
